# GB Lab Assessment

* [In-class quizzes](assessment/quizzes.md)
* [Final exam](assessment/final-exam.md)

Quizzes and exam are provided in MCQ format, where the stem is followed
by the alternatives, and the first alternative is the correct one.
Thus, these files are not meant to be directly used as assessment, and
must be used within a system that shuffles the alternatives.
